Why it’s different

Built to sit on one side of the table. Yours.

01

For you. Not from you.

A broker earns when you trade. A bank earns when you buy its products. A free chatbot earns when you never leave. Jarvis earns none of it. One objective: helping you build wealth over the long term, on principles that hold for decades.

02

Your whole picture. Not one question.

Most advice answers the question you asked. Jarvis looks at everything you share at once — what you own, what’s sitting idle, what you’re saving for — because the good answer usually isn’t in the question.
