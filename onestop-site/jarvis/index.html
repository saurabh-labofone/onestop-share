Why it’s different

Built to sit on one side of the table. Yours.

01

For you. Not from you.

A broker earns when you trade. A bank earns when you buy its products. A free chatbot earns when you never leave. Jarvis earns none of it. One objective: helping you build your wealth.

02

Your whole picture. Not one question.

Most advice answers the question you asked. Jarvis looks at everything at once — what you own, what’s sitting idle, what you’re saving for — because the good answer usually isn’t in the question.
